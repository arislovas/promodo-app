Download Promodo

Free. No account. The first time you open it, your computer asks for one confirmation – below is what to click.

Version 0.4.1, released 16 October 2026.

Mac

Which one? Click the Apple menu, then About This Mac. If “Chip” says Apple M1, M2, M3 or M4, pick the first. If it says “Processor … Intel”, pick the second.

Apple chip0.4.1, 143 MB Intel chip0.4.1, 150 MB

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Promodo into the Applications folder.

  2. Open Promodo from Applications. A window says Apple can’t check the app. Close it – that’s expected.

  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll down to Security.

  4. Next to the note about Promodo, click Open Anyway. Confirm with your Mac password or Touch ID.

  5. Click Open. Promodo starts. You won’t need to do this again.

Your Mac says the app is “damaged”?

Open the Terminal app, paste this line, press Enter, then open Promodo again:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Promodo.app

Windows

Windows 10 and 11. One file for every PC.

Windows0.4.1, 238 MB

  1. Run the downloaded Promodo-0.4.1-setup.exe. If your browser asks whether to keep the file, choose Keep.

  2. In the window “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Choose where to install and click Install. Promodo appears on the desktop and in the Start menu.

Example companies

Three made-up Lithuanian companies, drawn in full: functions, roles, IT systems and a diagram for every process. Use them to show Promodo to others, or to try things out before you draw your own. Names in the examples are in Lithuanian.

The sample used on this website.32 processes · 99 KB UAB „Aiški kryptis“Management and IT consulting: projects, sales, staff.30 processes · 90 KB

  1. Install Promodo 0.4.2 or later (above). Older versions can’t open example files.

  2. Download an example and double-click the downloaded .promodo file. If that doesn’t open Promodo, open Promodo, click the gear at the top right (Settings), then Open a package (.promodo)… and pick the file.

  3. Click Install. The company opens on Home: browse it as a user, or change anything in the Architect’s workspace. Your own libraries are not touched.

  4. To switch between companies, in the Architect’s workspace click the company name at the top left and choose one under Libraries on this computer.

Why does my computer ask?

Apple and Microsoft ask about every app that isn’t signed with a developer certificate yet. Promodo isn’t signed yet, so it needs one extra confirmation. That doesn’t mean the file is unsafe, and you can check that it is the original below.

Want to check the file wasn’t changed? Compare its SHA-256 code: on a Mac shasum -a 256 file, on Windows Get-FileHash file.
