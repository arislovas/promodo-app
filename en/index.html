Where to start?

I got a linkView only. Your manager sent you a folder or a file – you’ll have your company’s processes open in 5 minutes. I build processesYou draw the diagrams, keep the company structure and share it with colleagues.

From one process to the whole company

Every process has its own diagram, owner and manager. Together with the functions and roles they form one company library.

Sample company UAB „Kampo krautuvė“ (a corner shop chain), made with Promodo. Not a real company; names are in Lithuanian.

Draw without being stopped

Promodo marks BPMN mistakes and offers a fix, but never blocks drawing. Branches leave a gateway from its corners and lines go around steps; “Tidy up arrows” redraws the whole diagram in one step.

Have Bizagi files? Promodo opens them directly.

    The whole company in one library

    Functions, roles, processes with owner and manager, IT systems. A lane in a diagram is a role in the company structure, so names match everywhere and everyone sees where they take part.

    Pick a role to see its processes.

    Share it – colleagues only view

    The simplest way is a shared folder in OneDrive or Google Drive: colleagues always see the latest version. Without one, a single .promodo file is enough, and it can be password-protected.

    Only the architect who knows the company password can edit.

    Everyone finds their own work

    Find information searches every process, step and role. A cashier types “kasa” (till) and sees where they take part. A click opens the diagram.

    Try it on every step of the sample company.

      One diagram, six formats

      Images for clients, vector PDF, Word documentation, the Process description in Excel following the client’s template, and a BPMN 2.0 file for other tools. Each carries company, version and date.

      Moving from Bizagi?

      What comes across, what isn’t there yet, and what’s different.

      Comes across

      • .bpm and .bpmn files open directly
      • Layout, lanes and element types
      • Conditions, data and artifacts

      Not yet

      • Extended-attribute values
      • Automatic linking to the library – you link them yourself
      • Process simulation, or several people editing at once

      Different

      • Runs on Mac and Windows
      • Offline, no accounts
      • IT systems in ArchiMate notation next to the steps
